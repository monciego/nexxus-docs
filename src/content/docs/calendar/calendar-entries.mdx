---
title: Calendar Entries
description: Understand the types of calendar entries and how they are created or managed.
---

The calendar is made up of different kinds of entries, each serving a distinct
purpose.

## Types of entries

- **Events.** Scheduled productions and engagements, created and managed in the
  events area of the platform.
- **Rehearsals.** Preparation sessions linked to an event, scheduled in advance
  of the show.
- **Call sheet schedules.** Day-of timing pulled from a published
  [call sheet](/call-sheets/overview/), such as call times and locations.
- **Personal entries.** Your own commitments and reminders, which only you
  manage.

## How entries are created

Entries come from two places:

- **Generated from other parts of the platform.** Events, rehearsals, and call
  sheet schedules appear automatically once they are created and published
  elsewhere. You do not need to re-enter them.
- **Created directly.** Personal entries are added by you, giving you a place
  to track commitments that are not part of an event.

## Managing your personal schedule

Personal entries can be added, edited, and removed at any time, so your
calendar always reflects your real availability. Combined with the entries
generated from the rest of the platform, this keeps one complete view of your
time — see the [calendar overview](/calendar/overview/) for how it all comes
together.

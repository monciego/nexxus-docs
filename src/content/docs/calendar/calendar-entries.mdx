---
title: Calendar Entries
description: How to create and manage calendar entries.
---

Calendar entries allow users to add their own schedules to Nexxus. They can be used for meetings, reminders, appointments, or other activities that need to appear on the calendar.

## Creating a Calendar Entry

Users with the appropriate permission can create a calendar entry with the following information:

- **Title** — The name of the entry.
- **Description** _(Optional)_ — Additional information about the entry.
- **Location** _(Optional)_ — Where the activity will take place.
- **Color** — The color used to identify the entry on the calendar.
- **Start Date & Time** — When the entry begins.
- **End Date & Time** — When the entry ends.

## Adding Guests

Users can assign other users as guests to a calendar entry.

Assigned guests will see the entry on their own calendar, allowing everyone involved to keep track of the schedule.

## Managing Your Entries

Users can edit or delete the calendar entries they created.

Guests can view an entry assigned to them but do not automatically gain permission to modify or delete it.

## Viewing an Entry

Selecting a calendar entry opens its details, where you can view the information associated with the entry, including its schedule, location, description, and assigned guests.

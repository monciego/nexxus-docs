---
title: Calendar Overview
description: See events, rehearsals, and personal schedules together in one shared view.
---

The calendar brings everything happening on a given day into one view. Instead
of checking separate tools for events, rehearsals, and personal commitments,
you can see them side by side and plan around the full picture.

## Events and rehearsals

Events and their rehearsals appear on the calendar automatically, so the team
always knows what is scheduled and when. Rehearsals show up alongside the
events they belong to, making it easy to see preparation leading up to a show.

## Call sheet schedules

[Published call sheets](/call-sheets/sending-call-sheets/) contribute their
schedule to the calendar too. Call times and other schedule details from a call
sheet appear alongside the event, so the day-of plan is visible in context.

## Visibility by role and assignment

What you see in the calendar depends on your role and what you are assigned to.
The calendar shows the events, rehearsals, and call sheet schedules relevant to
you, keeping the view focused without exposing everything across the agency.

## Managing what you see

Individual [calendar entries](/calendar/calendar-entries/) — including your own
personal entries — let you keep the calendar accurate to your real schedule.

The **Clients** module allows teams to manage client relationships and keep their contact information organized.

Clients can be associated with events, making it easy to see which productions belong to each client and review their event history.

## Client List

The Clients page displays all registered clients and provides an overview of their information.

Each client also displays the number of events associated with them, allowing you to quickly see their activity in Nexxus.

Select a client to open their **Client Details** page.

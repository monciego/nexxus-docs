title: Clients Overview
description: An overview of clients in Nexxus and what a client profile contains.
---

Clients are the organizations or people who book events and talent through the
agency. They are the reason events happen — and the people you keep in touch
with throughout production.

Each client profile keeps two things together:

- **Contact information** — who to reach, and how.
- **Associated events** — everything that client has booked with the agency.

Because a client profile links contact details with their events, you always
have the context you need without hunting across the platform.

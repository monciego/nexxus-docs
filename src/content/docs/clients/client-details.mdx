title: Client Details
description: What you'll find on a client page in Nexxus.
---

The client page is the hub for everything connected to that client. Instead of
searching through separate parts of the platform, you can view a client's
information and activity in one place.

## What the client page contains

- **Contact information** — the client's company or organization details,
  primary contact, email address, and phone number.
- **Associated events** — all events the client has booked with the agency,
  with a way to jump into any of them.
- **Related activity** — supporting documents and communication tied to the
  client, such as call sheets and messages.

## Using the client page

The client page is designed to be the starting point for most client-related
work. Whether you're checking the details of an upcoming booking, reviewing
what events a client has on file, or catching up on recent communication,
everything connected to that client is available from here.

To add a client profile from scratch, see [Creating a Client](/clients/creating-a-client/).

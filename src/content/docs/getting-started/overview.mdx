Nexxus is a centralized management platform built for entertainment and talent
agencies. It brings together the people and work behind every production —
clients, talent, events, schedules, and communication — so your team can manage
every booking from a single place.

## Core areas of the platform

- **Events** — create and manage events, assign talent, and schedule rehearsals.
- **Clients** — manage client information and view their associated events.
- **Talents** — onboard talent, manage profiles and availability, and track assignments.
- **Call Sheets** — build, publish, and share call sheets with the right people.
- **Calendar** — view events, rehearsals, and schedules in one place.
- **Messages** — communicate with clients and talent through direct and group messages.
- **Media** — organize and manage production media.
- **My Profile** — manage your personal or talent profile information.

## A typical workflow

From booking to show day, a common flow looks like this:

1. Create a client in the platform.
2. Onboard the talent you'll be working with.
3. Create an event for the client.
4. Assign talent to the event.
5. Schedule rehearsals leading up to the event.
6. Build and send call sheets to everyone involved.
7. Communicate with clients and talent through messages as plans evolve.

Each step lives in its own section of the platform, so information stays
organized and easy to find throughout the production.

## Who uses Nexxus

- **Agency staff** coordinate events, manage clients and talent, and publish call sheets.
- **Clients** follow their events and communicate with the agency.
- **Talents** manage their profile, availability, and assignments.

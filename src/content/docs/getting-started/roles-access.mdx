---
title: Roles & Access
description: The typical roles in Nexxus and how access to features is controlled.
---

Nexxus gives each person in your organization a role that determines what they
can see and do in the platform. Understanding these roles helps you set up your
team and keep information accessible to the right people.

## Typical roles

- **Administrator** — has full control of the platform, including managing users,
  permissions, and system settings.
- **Agency staff** — managers, coordinators, and bookers who manage events,
  clients, talent, and call sheets as part of their day-to-day work.
- **Client** — views their events and communicates with the agency through the platform.
- **Talent** — manages their profile, availability, and assignments, and receives
  call sheets and updates for the events they're part of.

## How access is controlled

Access to features is controlled by permissions attached to each role. A role's
permissions determine which areas of the platform its members can view, create,
edit, or manage.

Administrators can review and customize permissions to fit how your agency
works. See [Roles & Permissions](/administration/roles-permissions/) for details
on roles and their permissions, and [Access Control](/administration/access-control/)
to learn how access is configured and managed.

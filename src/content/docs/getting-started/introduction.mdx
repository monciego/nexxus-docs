Nexxus is a centralized management platform built for entertainment and talent
agencies. It brings together the people and work behind every production — clients,
talent, events, schedules, and communication — in one place.

## What Nexxus covers

- **Events** — create and manage events, assign talent, schedule rehearsals, and track checklists.
- **Clients** — manage client information and view their associated events.
- **Talents** — manage talent profiles, approvals, assignments, and event-specific needs.
- **Call Sheets** — create, customize, publish, and share call sheets.
- **Calendar** — view events, rehearsals, and personal schedules in one place.
- **Messages** — communicate through direct and group messages and share call sheets.
- **Media** — organize and manage production media.
- **My Profile** — manage your personal or talent profile information.

## Who uses Nexxus

- **Agency staff** coordinate events, manage clients and talent, and publish call sheets.
- **Clients** follow their events and communicate with the agency.
- **Talents** manage their profile, availability, and assignments.

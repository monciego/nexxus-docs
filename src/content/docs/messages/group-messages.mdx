---
title: Group Messages
description: Coordinate with clients, talent, and staff together in group conversations.
---

Group Messages bring multiple people into a single conversation — for example,
everyone involved in an event or production. They keep clients, talent, and agency
staff aligned on the same thread instead of relying on separate one-to-one chats.

## Creating a group

Create a group for any team or production, such as an upcoming event, and give it
a name that everyone can recognize. The group becomes the shared space for that
effort, with all conversation kept in one history.

## Adding members

You can add members to a group at any time — agency staff, clients, and talent.
Everyone added can see the conversation and contribute to it. Members can also be
removed when they no longer need to be part of the thread.

## Coordinating together

Groups are ideal for coordinating with clients, talent, and staff at the same
time. Share updates, ask questions, and resolve issues in a single place so no one
is left out of the loop. You can also attach files, such as a published call
sheet, so the whole group sees the same information — see
[Sharing Call Sheets](/messages/sharing-call-sheets/).

For one-to-one conversations, see [Direct Messages](/messages/direct-messages/).

---
title: Sharing Call Sheets
description: How published call sheets are shared through messages.
---

Published call sheets can be shared through Nexxus Messages.

Users must have the **Send Call Sheets** permission to send a call sheet to a conversation.

## Sending a Call Sheet

A published call sheet can be sent through:

- A direct message.
- A group message.

The call sheet is shared within the conversation so authorized participants can access it without leaving the messaging interface.

## Access Control

Sharing a call sheet does not automatically grant access to users who are not authorized to view it.

If a user does not have access to the call sheet, they will not be able to open it even if it has been shared in their conversation.

This ensures that call sheet access continues to follow Nexxus permissions.

## Pinning Call Sheets

Multiple call sheets can be pinned within a conversation.

Pinned call sheets provide quick access to important production documents without requiring users to search through the conversation history.

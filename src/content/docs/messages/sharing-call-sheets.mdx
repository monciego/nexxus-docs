---
title: Sharing Call Sheets
description: Share published call sheets with direct and group message recipients.
---

Call sheets are the schedule everyone on a production relies on, and sharing them
through messages makes sure the right people see the right version. Once a call
sheet is published, you can attach it to a direct or group conversation so
everyone in the chat sees the same schedule.

## Attaching a call sheet

Open the direct or group conversation you want to share with, then attach the
published call sheet. Recipients can view the schedule right in the conversation,
without needing a separate link or download. For guidance on preparing and sending
call sheets, see [Sending Call Sheets](/call-sheets/sending-call-sheets/).

## Staying on the latest version

Call sheets are often updated as details change. When a shared call sheet is
updated, recipients stay on the latest version — the conversation always points to
the current schedule, so no one is left working from an outdated copy.

For an overview of what call sheets contain, see [Call Sheets](/call-sheets/overview/).

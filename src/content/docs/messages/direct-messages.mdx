---
title: Direct Messages
description: Communicate one-to-one with clients, talent, or agency staff.
---

Direct Messages are one-to-one conversations between two people on the platform —
a client, a talent, or agency staff. They are the quickest way to clarify details,
confirm arrangements, or follow up on a specific topic.

## Starting a conversation

You can start a direct message from a person's profile or from the Messages area.
Pick the person you want to reach, and the conversation opens right away. Messages
to someone outside your team are delivered to their Nexxus account, so everything
stays in one place.

## Attaching files

You can attach files to a message, such as contracts, signed forms, or reference
materials. Attachments are shared with the recipient as part of the conversation,
making it easy to review files without switching to another channel.

## Keeping a history

Every direct message is kept as a running history tied to the people involved.
The full thread — messages and attachments — remains available for review, so you
can refer back to earlier decisions or hand off the conversation context to a
colleague later.

For conversations involving more than two people, see [Group Messages](/messages/group-messages/).

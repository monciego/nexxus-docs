Media Management keeps all production media — photos, videos, and documents — in
one place. Instead of chasing files across devices and inboxes, your team can
upload, organize, and share media from a single library.

## Uploading media

Upload photos, videos, and documents to the media library as they come in from a
production. Files are stored centrally, so anyone with access can find them when
they are needed.

## Organizing media

Keep the library easy to navigate by organizing media into folders and tagging
items with labels such as the production, date, or content type. Folders and tags
make it fast to find a specific photo, clip, or contract later.

## Attaching media to events and talents

Media can be attached to the things it belongs to. Attach photos and videos to
the [event](/events/overview/) they were captured at, and link headshots, reels,
or documents to the [talent](/talents/overview/) profiles they relate to. This
keeps context with the media, so the right file is always one click away.

## Controlling who can view it

Administrators control which media is visible to which users. Sensitive documents
can be restricted to agency staff, while selected photos and videos can be shared
with clients and talent. Access is managed the same way as other parts of the
platform — see [Access Control](/administration/access-control/).

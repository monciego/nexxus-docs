title: Roles & Permissions
description: Understand the predefined roles and the permissions that come with them.
---

Roles bundle the permissions that determine what a user can see and do on the
platform. Nexxus ships with predefined roles that cover the typical ways people
work with the platform.

## Predefined roles

- **Administrator** — full access to the platform, including managing users,
  configuring access control, and changing settings.
- **Agency staff** — day-to-day access for coordinating events, clients, talent,
  call sheets, and communication.
- **Client** — access focused on following their events and communicating with
  the agency.
- **Talent** — access focused on their own profile, availability, and assignments.

## Reviewing and customizing permissions

Administrators can review which role can do what, so it is always clear what each
group of users can access. Permissions can also be customized to fit how your
agency works — for example, giving a specific role access to more or fewer
features than the default.

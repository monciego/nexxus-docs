---
title: Access Control
description: Control what each user can see and do across the platform.
---

Access Control gives administrators fine-grained control over what each user can
see and do — which events, clients, talents, media, and conversations are visible
to which roles. This keeps sensitive information in the right hands while making
sure people can do their jobs.

## How access is determined

Access is determined by two things working together:

- **Role** — the user's role defines the general permissions they hold, such as
  what they can create, edit, or manage. See [Roles & Permissions](/administration/roles-permissions/).
- **Assignments** — access to specific items, such as an individual event, client,
  or talent, is based on whether the user is assigned to it. A talent sees their
  own profile and assignments; agency staff see the events and clients they work on.

## Configuring access

Administrators configure access control for each area of the platform — events,
clients, talents, media, and messages — deciding which roles can view and act on
each type of item. You can also restrict specific items so only the people
involved can see them.

## Reviewing access

Because access is set per role and per assignment, it is worth reviewing from
time to time. As people join, leave, or change responsibilities, updating their
role and assignments keeps access accurate. See [Users](/administration/users/)
for managing accounts and their roles.

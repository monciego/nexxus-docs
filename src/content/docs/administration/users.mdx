Users are the accounts for everyone who uses Nexxus — agency staff, clients, and
talent. Managing users means inviting the right people, giving them the right
role, and keeping accounts active for as long as they are needed.

## Inviting users

New users are added to the platform by invitation. When you invite someone, they
receive an invitation they can accept to create their account and sign in. This
keeps access controlled: people can only join the platform when an administrator
invites them.

## Assigning roles

Each user is assigned a role that determines what they can see and do — such as
agency staff, Client, or Talent. Choose the role that matches how the person will
use the platform. You can change a user's role later as their involvement changes.
See [Roles & Permissions](/administration/roles-permissions/) for what each role
includes.

## Activating and deactivating accounts

Accounts can be activated or deactivated at any time. Deactivating an account
revokes the person's access without deleting their information, which is useful
when someone leaves the agency or finishes a project. Their account can be
reactivated if they return, with their history preserved.

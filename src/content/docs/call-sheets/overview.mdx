A call sheet is the day-of schedule for an event. It tells everyone involved who
needs to be where and when — call times, locations, and the contacts to reach
with questions. Instead of relying on scattered messages or word of mouth, a
single call sheet gives the whole team one source of truth for the day.

## The call sheet workflow

Call sheets follow a simple workflow that fits into the rest of the platform:

- **Build.** Start from a [call sheet template](/call-sheets/templates/) for a
  consistent layout, or build a call sheet from scratch.
- **Customize.** Add the event details, call times, locations, and any notes the
  team needs for the day.
- **Publish.** Once the details are final, publish the call sheet so it becomes
  the official version of record.
- **Share.** Send the published call sheet to the right people through Messages
  so everyone gets it in time.

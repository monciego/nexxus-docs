---
title: Sending Call Sheets
description: Share published call sheets with the right people through Messages and the calendar.
---

Once a call sheet is published, it needs to reach everyone involved. Nexxus
makes that easy by sharing call sheets through the same tools your team already
uses.

## Share through Messages

Send a call sheet through [Messages](/messages/sharing-call-sheets/) in a
direct or group conversation. Sharing it in a group conversation is a quick way
to reach an entire crew or department at once, while a direct message works
well for an individual.

## Recipients see the latest version

When someone receives a call sheet, they always see the latest published
version. If the schedule changes after it was shared, you can update and
republish the call sheet, and recipients will get the updated version — no one
is left working from an outdated schedule.

## Find call sheets in the calendar

Published call sheets also appear alongside their event in the
[calendar](/calendar/overview/), so the schedule is visible in context with
everything else happening on that day.

---
title: Sending Call Sheets
description: How published call sheets are downloaded and shared with participants.
---

Published call sheets can be shared with users through Nexxus Messages.

Only users with the **Send Call Sheets** permission can send call sheets.

## Download as PDF

After a call sheet has been published, it can be downloaded as a **PDF**.

This allows the call sheet to be saved, printed, or shared outside of Nexxus when needed.

## Send to a Conversation

Authorized users can use the **Send** action to share a published call sheet through:

- Direct messages
- Group messages

The call sheet becomes available to the recipients through the conversation.

## Event Participants

When a call sheet is sent through an event's group conversation, users assigned to the event can access the call sheet according to their existing permissions.

Nexxus prevents users from opening call sheets they are not authorized to access.

## Pin Call Sheets

Multiple call sheets can be pinned within a conversation.

Pinned call sheets remain easily accessible to participants without requiring them to search through the conversation history.

## Access Control

Sharing a call sheet does not bypass Nexxus permissions.

If a user does not have authorization to access a call sheet, they will not be able to open it even if the call sheet was shared in a conversation.

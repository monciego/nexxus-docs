title: Call Sheet Templates

---

Call sheet templates are reusable layouts that standardize how call sheets are
structured. They define the sections and arrangement used for every call sheet
created from them, so call times, locations, contacts, and notes always appear
in a familiar place.

## Creating and managing templates

Templates are built once and used many times:

- **Create a template** with the sections your events typically need, such as
  call times, locations, and contacts.
- **Organize templates** so teams can quickly find the right starting point for
  different types of events.
- **Update a template** when the standard layout changes; new call sheets will
  start from the updated version.

## Using a template

A template serves as the starting point for a new call sheet. Instead of
rebuilding the layout from scratch, you pick a template and fill in the details
for the specific event. This keeps call sheets consistent across the agency and
reduces the chance of missing a section.

See [Creating a Call Sheet](/call-sheets/creating-a-call-sheet/) for how to use
a template as the foundation of a new call sheet.

This page walks through bringing a new talent into Nexxus, from creating the
profile to moving the talent through approval.

## Steps

1. **Create the talent profile.** Add the talent's personal and professional
   details so the profile is ready to build on.
2. **Capture details and availability.** Record the information the agency
   needs to book the talent, including when they are available to work.
3. **Submit for approval.** Send the profile through the approval flow so the
   talent can be cleared to take on work.
4. **Record professional needs or requirements.** Note any special
   requirements to account for when assigning the talent to an event.

## The approval flow

Every talent profile has a status that reflects where it is in the process:

- **Pending** — the profile has been submitted and is awaiting review.
- **Approved** — the profile has been reviewed and the talent is cleared to
  take on work.

Only approved talents can be assigned to events. If a profile isn't approved,
keep it up to date and resubmit once the outstanding details are resolved.

Once approved, see [Talent Assignments](/events/talent-assignments/) to learn
how to attach the talent to an event.

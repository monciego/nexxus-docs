Talent onboarding is used to collect the information needed to create and manage a talent profile in Nexxus.

The onboarding process contains six steps:

1. Personal Information
2. Physical
3. Talents
4. Wardrobe
5. Portfolio
6. Performer Needs

Some fields may change depending on the selected sex.

## Personal Information

The Personal Information step collects the talent's basic information:

- **Stage Name**
- **Phone Number**
- **Date of Birth**
- **Sex**
- **Base Location**
- **Years of Experience**

## Physical Information

The Physical step collects measurements and clothing sizes.

### Body Measurements

- Height
- Weight
- Bust/Chest
- Waist
- Hips
- Inseam
- Outseam
- Head Size

### Clothing Sizes

- Shoe Size
- Pant Size
- Shirt Size

### Male Sizes

These fields are displayed when **Male** is selected:

- Suit Jacket Size

### Female Sizes

These fields are displayed when **Female** is selected:

- Dress Size
- Bra Size

### Tattoos

Talent can provide:

- Tattoo Description
- Link to Tattoo Photos

## Talents and Skills

The Talents step records the talent's skills, specialties, and areas of expertise.

At least one talent category must be selected.

Available categories include:

- Actor
- Model
- Musician
- Dancer
- Host
- Voice Actor
- Specialty Artist
- Costume & Wig Artist
- Production & Crew
- Administrative

Additional information can include:

- Specialties and Skills
- Languages
- Specialty Equipment and Skills

### Specialty Equipment

Additional fields are displayed when specific equipment or skills are selected.

For example:

- **Stilts** — Color of pant coverings
- **Aerial** — Apparatuses owned
- **Fire Performance** — Props used
- **Acrobatics** — Acrobatic skills

The Wardrobe step records the clothing and equipment available to the talent.

## Portfolio

The Portfolio step contains the talent's public-facing profile information.

This includes:

- **Profile Photo**
- **Bio**

### Social Media

Talent can provide links to their social media accounts:

- Instagram
- Facebook
- TikTok
- YouTube
- X

## Performer Needs

The Performer Needs step records information that may be important when working with the talent during an event.

### Dietary Restrictions

Talent can specify dietary requirements such as:

- Vegan
- Vegetarian
- Paleo
- Gluten-Free
- Lactose-Free
- Other

### Allergies

Talent can specify allergies including:

- Nuts
- Shellfish
- Dairy
- Eggs
- Soy
- Wheat
- Other

### Vocal and Green Room Needs

Talent can specify requirements such as:

- Tea
- Honey
- Lemon
- Warm Water
- Other

Additional notes and special requests can also be provided.

## Review and Submission

After completing the onboarding steps, the talent is taken to a final review page.

The talent can review all submitted information before submitting the profile for approval.

After submission, the profile status changes to <Badge text="Pending Review" variant="note" /> and is reviewed by the team.

## Approval

If the profile is approved, the talent receives an email notification and can begin using Nexxus.

Once approved, see [Talent Assignments](/events/talent-assignments/) to learn
how to attach the talent to an event.

## Rejection

If the profile is rejected, the reviewer must provide a reason or feedback explaining what needs to be corrected.

The talent can update the required information and submit the profile again for review.

## Client-Specific Onboarding

Nexxus provides a generic talent onboarding structure designed to cover common talent information.

Before deploying Nexxus for a client, the onboarding process can be reviewed against the client's existing workflow. Additional onboarding requirements may be configured when the client's requirements differ significantly from the standard fields.

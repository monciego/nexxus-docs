---
title: Talent Overview
description: An overview of talents in Nexxus and what a talent profile contains.
---

Talents are the performers managed by the agency. Their profiles keep
everything the agency needs to book and support them.

Each talent profile captures:

- **Personal and professional details** — who the talent is and what they do.
- **Approval status** — whether the talent is approved to work on events.
- **Assignments to events** — which events the talent is attached to.
- **Event-specific needs** — any requirements a talent has for a particular
  event, so nothing is missed during production.

Keeping talent profiles complete makes it easier to match the right performer
to the right event.

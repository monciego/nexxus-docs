The **My Profile** page allows talents to view and manage their own profile information.

The information displayed on the profile comes from the talent's onboarding data.

## Profile Information

Talents can review and update their profile information, including their personal details, skills, measurements, wardrobe, portfolio, and performer needs.

Keeping this information updated helps ensure that event teams have accurate information when assigning talents to productions.

## Gallery

The profile also includes a **Gallery** where talents can upload and manage their photos.

The gallery provides a visual collection of the talent's work and profile images.

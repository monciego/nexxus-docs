My Profile is the talent-facing view of a profile. It lets talents manage their
own information and see their status with the agency, without needing to go
through agency staff for every update.

## What talents can do from My Profile

- **Manage their profile** — update personal information, availability, and
  professional details directly.
- **See their approval status** — check whether the profile is pending or
  approved at a glance.
- **View event assignments** — see which events they are attached to.
- **Receive call sheets** — get the call sheets for the events they're assigned
  to.

## Keeping the profile current

A complete, up-to-date profile helps the agency match the right opportunities
to each talent. Talents should keep their availability and professional details
current, and reach out to the agency if anything about their approval status
looks wrong.

To understand how profiles are set up and approved, see [Talent Onboarding](/talents/talent-onboarding/).

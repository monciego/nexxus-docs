---
title: Talent Assignments
description: How to attach talent to an event and manage confirmations and event-specific needs.
---

Talent assignments connect talent profiles to an event. They define who is involved, whether they've confirmed, and what they need for this specific engagement.

## Assigning talent

You can attach one or more talents to an event directly from the event. Each talent is drawn from your agency's talent profiles, so everyone assigned to an event is someone you already manage.

## Approval and confirmation

Once talent is assigned, the assignment moves through a confirmation flow:

- The talent is notified of the assignment.
- The talent accepts or confirms their participation.
- The confirmation status is reflected on the event so your team knows who is locked in.

This keeps the whole agency in sync about who has committed to the event.

## Event-specific needs

For each assigned talent, you can capture needs that apply to this event only, such as:

- **Call time** — when the talent needs to arrive.
- **Wardrobe** — clothing or styling requirements.
- **Travel** — transportation or accommodation arrangements.
- **Special requirements** — anything else the talent needs for the engagement.

## Managing talent profiles

Talent assignments draw on your talent profiles. To add or update the talent you can assign, see [Talents Overview](/talents/overview/).

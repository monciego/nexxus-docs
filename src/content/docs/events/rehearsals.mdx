Rehearsals allow event teams to schedule and coordinate preparation sessions before an event.

Rehearsals are created from the Event Details page and are associated with a specific event.

## Creating a Rehearsal

A rehearsal can contain the following information:

- **Title** — The name of the rehearsal.
- **Description** _(Optional)_ — Additional information about the rehearsal.
- **Start Date & Time** — When the rehearsal begins.
- **End Date & Time** _(Optional)_ — When the rehearsal ends.
- **Location** _(Optional)_ — Where the rehearsal takes place.
- **Notes** _(Optional)_ — Additional information or instructions.
- **Video Link** _(Optional)_ — A link for an online rehearsal or related video meeting.
- **Status** — The current state of the rehearsal.

Available statuses are:

- <Badge text="Scheduled" variant="note" />
- <Badge text="Completed" variant="success" />
- <Badge text="Cancelled" variant="danger" />

## Rehearsal Attendees

Attendees are selected from the talents assigned to the event.

When adding an attendee, you can also specify their **role for the rehearsal**.

This allows the same talent to have different responsibilities depending on the rehearsal.

## Managing Rehearsals

Users with the appropriate permissions can:

- View rehearsal details.
- Create rehearsals.
- Edit rehearsals.
- Delete rehearsals.

Rehearsal dates are also included in the event timeline and appear in the Calendar.

## Rehearsals in the Calendar

Rehearsals automatically appear in the [Calendar](/calendar/overview/) alongside events and other calendar entries.

Because rehearsals have their own start and end dates, the calendar gives your team a complete view of both the event itself and the preparation leading up to it.

This makes it easier to see rehearsals, events, and other scheduled activities together.

---
title: Rehearsals
description: How to schedule, manage, and cancel rehearsals against an event in Nexxus.
---

Rehearsals are scheduled against an event to practice and prepare before the big day. They keep the talent and crew aligned on what needs to happen and when.

## Creating a rehearsal

When you create a rehearsal for an event, you provide:

- **Date and time** — when the rehearsal takes place.
- **Location** — where it will be held.
- **Attached talent** — the talent who needs to attend.

The rehearsal is automatically linked to its event, so it stays connected to the planning that surrounds it.

## Updating a rehearsal

Plans change, and rehearsals can be updated after they're created. You can adjust the date, time, location, or the talent attached — and everyone involved will see the latest information.

## Cancelling a rehearsal

If a rehearsal is no longer needed, you can cancel it. It will be removed from the event and from everyone's schedules.

## Rehearsals in the calendar

Rehearsals appear in the [Calendar](/calendar/overview/) alongside events and personal schedules, so your team can see the full picture of what's happening on any given day — including preparation time, not just show time.
